# One pool: the health check shares the tiles' connections

14 ***October*** 2026, ***evening***. The ***maintainer*** ***asked*** for a ***single*** ***database*** ***pool***, and for the ***health*** ***check*** to ***work*** another ***way***, with ASP.NET Core or Nginx.

## The decision

The ***service*** ***keeps*** ***one*** ***pool***. `/health` ***stays*** an ASP.NET Core ***health*** ***check***, and its ***ping*** ***waits*** in the ***pool***'s ***queue*** for ***one*** ***second*** at ***most***. Nginx ***cannot*** ***take*** the ***job***: its ***free*** ***edition*** ***watches*** ***only*** the ***requests*** it ***passes*** on, and ***sees*** the ***service***, ***never*** the ***database*** ***behind*** it.

## What changed

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Two pools: the tiles' pool, and a pool of one connection named `tessera-health` | ***One*** ***pool***, ***named*** `tessera` |
| A busy tile pool left `/health` at `UP` | A ***pool*** ***busy*** for more than a ***second*** ***makes*** `/health` ***say*** `DOWN` |
| N-26 c3: the ping never waits behind tiles | N-26 c3: the ***ping*** ***gives*** ***up*** ***within*** ***about*** a ***second***, and ***leaves*** the ***queue*** |

The ***ping*** ***now*** ***carries*** a ***cancel*** ***signal*** that fires ***after*** ***one*** ***second***. It ***takes*** the ***ping*** out of the ***queue***, so a ***ping*** that ***gave*** ***up*** ***never*** ***takes*** a ***connection*** a ***tile*** is ***waiting*** for. The ***ping*** ***keeps*** its ***own*** one-second ***clock*** as ***well***, ***since*** a ***frozen*** ***database*** can ***hold*** Npgsql's ***cancel*** for ***longer***.

`Database.cs` ***holds*** 147 ***lines***, as ***before***: the ***second*** ***pool***'s ***lines*** ***went***, and the ***cancel*** ***signal*** ***took*** their ***place***. The ***gain*** is ***one*** ***pool*** to ***reason*** ***about***, and no ***second*** ***set*** of ***connections*** in `pg_stat_activity`.

## What it costs

`/health` ***says*** `DOWN` ***while*** every ***connection*** ***stays*** ***busy*** for over a ***second***, ***though*** the ***database*** is ***well***. N-25 ***asks*** `/health` to ***say*** `UP` ***only*** when the ***service*** can ***serve*** ***tiles***, and such a ***service*** ***cannot*** ***serve*** a ***new*** ***tile*** ***within*** a ***second***, so the ***answer*** ***fits*** the ***story***. ***Nothing*** ***restarts*** the ***service*** on `DOWN` ***today***; `npm start` ***only*** ***waits*** for `UP`. A ***watcher*** that ***restarts*** on `DOWN` would ***need*** to ***allow*** for ***busy*** ***hours***.

## Tests

`PoolTests` ***now*** ***holds*** the ***only*** ***connection*** for ***three*** ***seconds*** and ***expects*** the ***ping*** to ***give*** ***up*** ***within*** a ***second*** and a ***half***, then to ***answer*** ***once*** the ***hold*** ***ends***. Every ***test*** ***passes***: 47 ***unit*** ***tests*** and 38 ***integration*** ***tests***.
